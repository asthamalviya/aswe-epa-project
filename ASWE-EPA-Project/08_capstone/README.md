# Capstone: Integrated Project

## Module Overview
The Capstone is your integrated project that brings together learning from all modules. It demonstrates your ability to lead a complex business technology initiative from concept through implementation.

## Purpose
- Integrate knowledge across all modules
- Demonstrate leadership and strategic thinking
- Show real business value and outcomes
- Evidence professional practice and reflection

## KSB Focus Area: ksb-core
**Core Capabilities:**
- Strategic project leadership
- Business case development
- Stakeholder management
- Outcomes realization
- Professional judgment and reflection

## What to Include
- **Business Case:** Problem definition, proposed solution, benefits (£ terms)
- **Project Plan:** Timeline, resources, risks, mitigation
- **Outcomes:** What you delivered, lessons learned, organizational impact
- **Reflection:** What you'd do differently, how you've grown

## How to Fill This In
1. Review capstone feedback from examiners
2. Identify main gaps from feedback
3. Create business case with quantified benefits
4. Show how you led the project end-to-end
5. Document actual outcomes and learnings
6. Add screenshots showing project progress, outcomes, team feedback

## Note
Capstone is typically worth more than individual modules. Focus on:
- Integration (how does this bring modules together?)
- Leadership (evidence of leading others)
- Outcomes (what actually happened vs. what you proposed?)
- Reflection (lessons learned and growth)
