# Quick Template Guide for Modules 3-7

Since Module 1 & 2 are complete references, use them as templates for Modules 3-7.

## How to Fill Each Module

### For Each KSB Folder, Create 3 Files:

**1. details.md** (Template)
```markdown
# [KSB-CODE]: [Name]

## What This KSB Is About
[One-line explanation - what is the core skill?]

## Why It Matters
[Why do employers care about this?]

## What Examiners Want to See
[3-4 bullet points of what evidence is needed]

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** [From feedback]
**Feedback:** "[Quote from feedback]"
**To Improve:** [What held you back?]

## Related Files
- evidence-submitted.md
- evidence-new.md
- Screenshots folder
```

**2. evidence-submitted.md** (Template)
```markdown
# [KSB] Evidence - What You Submitted

## Your Submission Summary
[What did you show in your PDF?]

## Examiner's Feedback
[Quote from feedback]

## Strengths
[Bullet list of what they liked]

## Gaps to Fill
[Bullet list of what they wanted more of]

## Add Screenshots
[List recommended screenshots]
```

**3. evidence-new.md** (Template)
```markdown
# [KSB] New Evidence - [Gap Topic]

## The Gap: [What You Need to Show]
[Clear statement of what's missing]

## Evidence: [Main Topic]

[Your detailed explanation with examples]

### Example
[Concrete scenario showing how to address gap]

## Simple Summary for Examiner
*"[Your 3-4 sentence elevator pitch addressing the gap]"*

## What to Add to This Folder
[List of screenshots/evidence needed]
```

**Per Module:** 3-4 hours to fill 4-6 KSBs

- Write evidence-new.md for each KSB: 45 min/KSB
- Add screenshots: 15 min/KSB

## Getting Help

Each KSB needs:
1. **Gap identification** - What did examiners ask for?
2. **Evidence generation** - What would answer that gap?
3. **Articulation** - How do you explain it simply?

Use Module 1 & 2 as your complete reference examples for structure and depth.
