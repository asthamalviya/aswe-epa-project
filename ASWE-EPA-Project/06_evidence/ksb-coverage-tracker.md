Two shared criteria cover it (K8, S7, B4, B6, B7 and K9, K10, S8), so one piece of evidence showing a real team, a named leadership or management theory, your CPD and something you shared closes most of it.
2. **Add sustainability and accessibility evidence** (K20, B8). K20 is the only AM2 Distinction criterion with no evidence at all. Evidence your team's green computing choices (for example AWS right-sizing, region or instance selection in M6) and how you made a solution accessible (WCAG, GDS service standard).
3. **Rewrite key passages in the first person.** None of the seven submissions consistently uses "I"; M5 and M7 never do. The assessor cannot credit your actions if the evidence says "the PoC" or "we". Before the professional discussion, prepare a first-person account of your role in each module.
4. **Find a real change-of-priorities story** (S20 Pass and Distinction). Describe when priorities changed, the revised recommendation you made, how it shaped the team's plan, and compare it with another way you could have responded.
5. **Justify standards, not just name them** (K19, S15 Distinction). For each law or standard, explain why it applies and what you did differently because of it, for technical and non-technical audiences.
6. **Fix internal contradictions before the assessor reads the portfolio** (portfolio can be refined at any point before EPA):
   - M2: feature importance text contradicts its table; KPI values headed "Example"; leftover AI-generated text ("Here is a visual representation of your...").
   - M4: ingestion time 6 hours vs 3–5 days; Oracle in text vs PostgreSQL build; EU GDPR cited.
   - M5: weighted decision matrix totals do not recalculate; "no data leaves CH" vs GPT-4o on Azure; reference placeholders "Accessed: [date]".
   - M6: Python 3.9 vs 3.12; two vs three adaptation events; ALB vs API Gateway; deployment claimed and also recommended.
   - M7: "0 critical gaps" vs Gap 5 not addressed; v0 size 90 vs 106 lines.
   - M1: "How the proposal aligns" slide pairs goals with the wrong justifications; DEI deadlines already passed.
7. **Save the missing Multiverse pages** for K8, K13, B1, K21, K22, K23, K28, S20 and S21, so the standard wording can be checked (B1 ratings are provisional).
