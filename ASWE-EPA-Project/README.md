## Assessment Methods

- **AM1: Capstone Project**: 6,000-word report (plus or minus 10%), then a 30-minute presentation and 30 minutes of questions
- **AM2: Portfolio**: 60-minute professional discussion underpinned by your portfolio (no word count)

Each method is graded Fail, Pass or Distinction. Two Passes give a Pass overall, one Distinction gives a Merit, two Distinctions give a Distinction.

## KSB Notation

K = Knowledge, S = Skills, B = Behaviours. Each KSB is assessed in one method only. Full wording and Pass/Distinction criteria: `.kiro/steering/ksb-quick-reference.md`.
