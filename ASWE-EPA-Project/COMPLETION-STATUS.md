## How to Use Module 1 as Your Template

### For Any KSB, Copy This Pattern:

**Step 1: Create details.md**
- Look at `01_module-1/ksb-k7/details.md`
- Adapt the structure for your KSB
- Keep: "What This KSB Is About", "Why It Matters", "What Examiners Want to See"
- Change: The specific content to match your KSB

**Step 2: Create evidence-submitted.md**
- Look at `01_module-1/ksb-k7/evidence-submitted.md`
- Adapt structure for your KSB
- Include: Your submission summary, examiner feedback, strengths, gaps
- Add: Screenshots section

**Step 3: Create evidence-new.md**
- Look at `01_module-1/ksb-k7/evidence-new.md` (longest, most detailed)
- This is your main evidence piece
- Structure: The Gap → Evidence → Examples → Simple Summary
- Include: Tables, comparisons, detailed explanations

**Step 4: Add Screenshots**
- Extract from your PDF
- Name clearly: `governance-framework.png`, etc.
- Reference in evidence-new.md
