# K10: Recognize Business Value Creation Through Data Strategy

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how data initiatives translate into actual business benefits - not just implementing tech for its own sake, but ensuring it creates measurable value (profit, efficiency, risk reduction).

## Why It Matters
Many organizations implement data strategies that look good on paper but don't deliver value. K10 is about business acumen - linking data decisions to real £ impact.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Quantified business benefits (not vague claims)
- Clear link between data/tech choices and business outcomes
- ROI calculations and payback periods
- Understanding of multiple value types (revenue, cost savings, risk reduction)
