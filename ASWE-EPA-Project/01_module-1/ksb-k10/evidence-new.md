- Risk reduction: £147k

- Payback: 10 weeks (still strong)

**Conclusion: Even at 50% accuracy, business case is rock-solid.**

## How to Customize This for Your Actual Situation

Replace the numbers with yours:
- **Current staff time on reports:** Ask finance/IT: "How many hours/month on reporting?" × hourly rate = your £X
- **Current error costs:** Look at past incidents: "How many last year? Cost per incident?"
- **Revenue opportunity:** Ask sales: "If we could respond 3 days faster, how much revenue would we capture?"
- **Risk incidents:** Audit/compliance team: "How many violations/breaches last year? Cost per incident?"

Use actual company numbers, not generic examples. That's what makes it credible.
