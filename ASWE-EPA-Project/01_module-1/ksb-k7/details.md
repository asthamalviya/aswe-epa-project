# K7: Demonstrate Up-to-Date Knowledge of Data Governance Frameworks

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding and explaining current best practices in data governance - how organizations structure rules, policies, and decision-making around data management.

## Why It Matters
Data governance is the foundation for reliable data strategy. Without clear frameworks, organizations make inconsistent decisions and lose control of data quality/security.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Knowledge of specific governance frameworks (not generic)
- Comparison of different governance models
- Understanding of how governance supports business goals
- Recognition of trade-offs and limitations
