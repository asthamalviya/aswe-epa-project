## Evidence: Technology Choice Analysis with KPI Mapping

### Your Technology Decision: Power BI for Data Dashboards

**Strategic Context:**
- Business Goal: "Enable data-driven decision-making across all departments"
- Current State: Excel spreadsheets + manual reporting
- Problem: Decisions delayed 3-5 days while reports are built

### Option A: Power BI (Your Proposed Choice)

**Strategic Benefits:**
- ✓ Integrates with Microsoft stack (Teams, Excel, Azure)
- ✓ Real-time dashboards (decisions made same day, not next week)
- ✓ Supports 500+ users without slowdown
- ✓ Easy for non-technical staff to use

**Strategic Drawbacks:**
- ✗ Learning curve for 100+ staff (costs time)
- ✗ Requires Azure infrastructure knowledge
- ✗ £40k/year licensing cost

**KPI Mapping (How It Achieves Strategy):**

| Business Goal | KPI | Measurement | Target | Power BI Impact |
|---------------|-----|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| Data-driven decisions | Decision speed | Days from request to decision | <1 day | Reduces from 4 days to <1 (real-time) |
| User adoption | Dashboard usage | % of staff using weekly | >70% | UI-friendly → targets 75% adoption |
| Decision quality | Variance in decisions | Do different teams make similar choices? | ±5% variance | Consistent data source → ±3% variance |
| Cost efficiency | Cost per user | $ per annual user | <£200 | £40k / 250 users = £160/user |

**Why Power BI Achieves Strategy:**
*"Our strategy prioritizes speed and consistency. Power BI's real-time dashboards achieve <1 day decision speed vs current 4 days, enabling faster market response. Microsoft integration reduces training burden, targeting 75% staff adoption. Consistent data source reduces decision variance from ±5% to ±3%, increasing confidence in decisions. Cost per user (£160) is acceptable given productivity gains."*

*"While Tableau excels at visualization, our primary strategic goal is speed and adoption, not aesthetics. Power BI's £80k lower cost and Microsoft integration directly support these goals. Tableau would achieve better visualization (useful for executive presentations) but at cost of slower decisions (strategy misalignment). We choose Power BI because it better moves us toward our stated KPIs."*

### Option C: Custom Dashboard (In-House)

**Strategic Benefits:**
- ✓ Complete control
- ✓ No vendor lock-in
- ✓ Customizable for exact needs

**Strategic Drawbacks:**
- ✗ 6-month build time (delays strategy realization)
- ✗ Requires 2 developers (£120k/year ongoing)
- ✗ Maintenance burden
- ✗ Can't keep up with changing tech

**Why Rejected Against Strategy:**
*"Custom build would achieve personalization but delays achieving decision-speed goal by 6 months. Strategic opportunity is being missed while we build. Commercial tools like Power BI achieve 95% of custom functionality at 20% of cost. Rejected because time-to-value misaligns with business urgency."*

---

## Strategic Trade-off Discussion

### Trade-off 1: Cost vs Speed
*"We chose Power BI (£40k/year) over custom build (£120k/year ongoing) because the strategic priority is decision speed, not total cost. The £40k investment pays back in 1 month through faster decisions leading to faster product iteration. In growth phase, speed > cost."*

### Trade-off 2: Standardization vs Flexibility
*"Power BI's Microsoft integration provides standardization (everyone uses same platform, consistent data), while Tableau offered more flexibility. We prioritized standardization because inconsistent decision-making (when different teams use different tools) costs more than lost flexibility."*

### Trade-off 3: Short-term Adoption vs Long-term Learning
*"Power BI's familiar Excel-like interface achieves fast adoption (targets 75% in 3 months), enabling quick strategy realization. Tableau would have slower adoption but deeper long-term skills. Given 12-month strategy cycle, fast adoption wins."*

## Strategic Alignment Summary

### Business Strategy: "Enable Data-Driven Decision-Making Across Organization"

**How Power BI Aligns:**

1. **Speed Component**
   - Current bottleneck: 4-day reporting cycle
   - Power BI solution: Real-time dashboards
   - Strategic impact: Decisions made same day, enabling competitive response

2. **Consistency Component**
   - Current problem: Teams use different data (inconsistent decisions)
   - Power BI solution: Single source of truth
   - Strategic impact: ±5% variance → ±3% consistency (better decision outcomes)

3. **Adoption Component**
   - Current gap: 40% staff never use reports
   - Power BI solution: Familiar Microsoft interface, mobile-ready
   - Strategic impact: 40% → 75% adoption (more of org enabled)

4. **Cost Component**
   - Strategic constraint: Limited IT budget
   - Power BI solution: £160 per user vs £240 (Tableau) vs £480 (custom)
   - Strategic impact: Achieves 95% of capability at lowest cost

### Strategic KPI Achievement

*"Power BI directly supports our strategic KPIs:*
- *Decision speed: 4 days → <1 day (400% improvement)*
- *Staff adoption: 40% → 75% participation (87% improvement)*
- *Decision consistency: ±5% variance → ±3% (60% improvement)*
- *Cost efficiency: £160/user (competitive)*

*These improvements enable our business strategy of faster product iteration and better-informed decisions. Technology choice directly supports strategic goals, not just operational efficiency."*
