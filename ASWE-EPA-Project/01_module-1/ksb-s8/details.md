# S8: Apply Strategic Thinking to Data and Technology Alignment

## What This KSB Is About
Ability to think strategically about how data and technology choices support or hinder organizational goals.

## Why It Matters
Many organizations have great tech but it doesn't align with business strategy. S8 is about making choices that actually move the needle toward business objectives.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Show how technology choices support specific business goals
- Identify misalignments and propose fixes
- Think about future needs and scaling
- Show understanding of strategic implications
