# K9: Understand Leadership Principles in Change Management

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how to lead and manage organizational change, especially when introducing new data/technology initiatives that affect how people work.

## Why It Matters
Technical solutions fail without good change management. K9 is about recognizing people resistance, communicating benefits, and bringing teams along during transformation.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Specific leadership principles applied (not generic)
- Recognition of change challenges (resistance, training needs, adoption barriers)
- Communication and engagement strategies
- Evidence of stakeholder involvement in decisions

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** B+
**Feedback:** "Demonstrated clear communication and some leadership principles. To improve: Deepen critical evaluation and show how leadership principles specifically address resistance or adoption challenges."
