# Capstone Project (AM1)

**What it is:** Assessment Method 1 of the End Point Assessment. A standalone report about a real work project from your job, followed by a presentation and questions. It is **not** tied to Module 7 or any other module; modules supply the knowledge you draw on.
**Weighting:** 50% of the EPA. Graded Fail, Pass or Distinction against the 25 AM1 KSBs.
**Your project:** AI knowledge search for software delivery teams (see `capstone-proposal-draft.md`).

---

## The two parts of AM1

| Part | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Report** | 6,000 words ±10% (5,400 to 6,600), submitted as **.docx** by week 12 of the EPA period | This folder |
| **Presentation and questions** | 30-minute presentation, then 30 minutes of questions (up to 10% buffer), after the report is submitted | `presentation/` (see `presentation/ABOUT-PRESENTATION.md`) |

## Report requirements (Multiverse "Structuring Your Project")

**Mandatory sections, in order:** project title; contents; summary; introduction; project scope; project plan; analysis and problem solving; research and findings; project outcomes (planning and analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, maintenance); recommendations and conclusions; references; appendix.

**Mandatory appendix items:**
- **KSB map**, as the **first appendix**: one row per AM1 KSB, one column per report section, with page numbers
- **Word count table**: total; minus coversheet, contents and diagram labels; minus everything from "end of report"; final count; ±10% range
- **Employer declaration**: signed by you and your manager, confirming the work is yours and done in your job

**Word count excludes:** appendices, references and diagrams.
**Your responsibility:** the report must be your own work and reflect your own role, even if the project was a team effort. Cite any AI use in the references (Multiverse gives a format for this).
