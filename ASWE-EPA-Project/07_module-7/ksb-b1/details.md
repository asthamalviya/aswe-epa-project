# B1: Takes Responsibility for Delivering Professional Standards of Work

## What This KSB Is About
Personal accountability for quality, deadlines, and professionalism. Not just completing tasks, but owning the outcome and ensuring work meets professional standards. Includes managing own time, asking for help when needed, and admitting mistakes.

## Why It Matters
Technical skills alone don't make professionals. B1 is about character: reliability, honesty, and commitment to excellence even when no one's watching.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Specific examples of taking responsibility for outcomes
- Evidence of managing workload and deadlines
- Examples of asking for help appropriately
- Admitting mistakes and taking corrective action
- Impact on project/team/business (consequences of your accountability)
