# K8: Organisational Governance and Compliance

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how organisations maintain control over technology, ensure regulatory compliance, and manage risk. Includes governance frameworks, compliance reporting, audit trails, and stakeholder communication.

## Why It Matters
Large organizations need governance to prevent chaos. K8 is about balancing control (preventing problems) with agility (moving fast). Too much governance slows everything; too little creates risk.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Knowledge of governance frameworks (COBIT, ISO 27001, SOX depending on industry)
- Compliance requirements understanding (regulatory, contractual, policy)
- Audit trail and evidence collection
- Stakeholder communication and reporting
- Evidence from actual governance implementation
