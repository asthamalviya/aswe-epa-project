# K21: Software Architecture and Design Patterns

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how to design large systems with scalability, maintainability, and resilience in mind. Includes architectural patterns (microservices, layered, event-driven), design principles (SOLID), and trade-offs between different approaches.

## Why It Matters
Good architecture scales to millions of users. Poor architecture breaks at thousands. K21 is about making intentional choices, not accident-driven design.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Knowledge of architectural patterns (microservices, monolith, serverless, event-driven)
- Understanding of SOLID principles and when to apply them
- Scalability and resilience considerations
- Trade-off analysis (complexity vs. benefit)
- Evidence from actual system design decisions
