### Milestones
1. **Week 3:** current-state analysis of quality practice in your organisation, located in the SDLC; at least two quality risks with business impact.
2. **Week 6:** evaluation of a specific system's design (patterns, coupling); architectural decision record (ADR) with at least two justified improvements and an outline testing approach.
3. **Week 10:** implement the improvements, run the testing strategy (automated, black-box, white-box, boundary analysis), integrate with CI/CD, final report with critical evaluation and evidence of stakeholder communication.

### What reaches an A (from the rubric)
- **LO1:** critically evaluates current practice against professional standards and emerging approaches (e.g. shift-left testing, architectural review); original insight into the organisation's quality position.
- **LO2:** an ADR showing professional judgement, e.g. where a pattern adds unnecessary complexity, where deferring a decision is sound, explicit trade-offs between competing design forces.
- **LO3:** evaluates the whole testing strategy: what it proved, what it missed, what the results mean for quality in the organisation.
- **LO4:** evaluates the communication and collaboration strategy as a whole: what worked, what you would change, how it reflects senior-practitioner standards.
