# Portfolio: Evidence Collection & Reflection

## Module Overview
The Portfolio is your collection of best evidence from across all modules, organized to show your professional development and mastery of KSBs.

## Purpose
- Showcase strongest evidence across all modules
- Show breadth and depth of professional practice
- Demonstrate reflection and continuous improvement
- Evidence professional standard achievement

## KSB Focus Area: ksb-core
**Core Capabilities:**
- Professional judgment
- Evidence of standards achievement
- Reflection on practice
- Integration across modules
- Continuous professional development

## Structure
```
09_portfolio/
├── README.md (this file)
├── ksb-core/
│   ├── details.md
│   ├── evidence-submitted.md
│   ├── evidence-new.md
│   └── [screenshots]
├── evidence-map.md (links to best evidence per KSB)
├── reflection.md
└── professional-development.md
```

## What to Include
- **Evidence Map:** Where to find best evidence for each KSB (links to module folders)
- **Reflection:** What you've learned, how you've developed, what you'd do differently
- **Professional Development:** Growth trajectory, new skills gained, how you'll continue learning
- **Integration:** How evidence across modules shows holistic professional capability

## How to Fill This In
1. Review all your module work
2. Identify 2-3 strongest pieces of evidence per KSB
3. Write a reflection piece on your professional journey
4. Create links between evidence items showing patterns/integration
5. Document professional development path

## Note
Portfolio is less about individual KSBs and more about:
- **Breadth:** Can you evidence all required KSBs?
- **Depth:** Do you show sophisticated understanding, not just tick-box compliance?
- **Integration:** Do you connect learning across modules?
- **Reflection:** Can you analyze your own performance objectively?
- **Growth:** Do you show development and learning from mistakes?

## Tips for Stronger Portfolio
1. **Show progression:** Start of module vs. end (how have you improved?)
2. **Show breadth:** Different types of evidence (reports, meetings, artefacts, feedback)
3. **Show depth:** Not just "I did X" but "I did X and learned Y about myself"
4. **Show integration:** Connect evidence across modules (e.g., "Module 1 governance thinking informed Module 4 project management")
5. **Be honest:** Reflect on challenges and what you'd do differently (shows maturity)
