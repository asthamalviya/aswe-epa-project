# Your Answers: one sheet to fill the evidence gaps

The evidence drafts contain 369 `[EVIDENCE NEEDED]` placeholders, but most ask the same few things. Answer the questions below (short notes are fine) and the answers can be written into every draft that needs them. Leave anything you cannot answer truthfully blank.

## 1. You and your role
1. Your job title, and how long you have held it.
2. Your team: its size, roles and what it builds or runs.
3. What you lead, formally or informally.

## 4. People and behaviour (K8, K9, S7, B2, B6)
