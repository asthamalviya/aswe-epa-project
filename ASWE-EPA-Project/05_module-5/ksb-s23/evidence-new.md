## Lessons Learned Documentation

### Lesson 1: Business Case Development
**What We Learned:**
- Early business case (Week 1) prevented scope creep
- Updated business case monthly (tracked against reality)
- Cost-benefit focus kept team aligned

**For Next Project:**
- Require formal business case in project charter
- Monthly business case review (compare forecast vs actual)
- Template: [Link to template created from this project]

---

### Lesson 2: Stakeholder Analysis Timing
**What We Learned:**
- Identifying stakeholders Day 1 vs Week 3 = massive difference
- Early engagement prevented 2 false starts
- Power/Interest matrix helped prioritize engagement

**For Next Project:**
- Stakeholder analysis is prerequisite to requirements
- Create power/interest matrix before requirements gathering
- Monthly stakeholder re-analysis (priorities change)

---

### Lesson 3: Requirements Gathering Method
**What We Learned:**
- Workshops better than interviews (cross-functional dialogue)
- Remote workshops need facilitator + scribe (dual roles failed)
- Documented requirements in user story format (easier for team)

**For Next Project:**
- Default to workshops for complex requirements
- Allocate 2 people per workshop (facilitator + scribe)
- Adopt user story format for all requirements documentation
