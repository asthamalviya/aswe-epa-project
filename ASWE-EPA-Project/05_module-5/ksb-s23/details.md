# S23: Business Analysis with Organizational Learning

## What This KSB Is About
Skill in applying BA methodologies while building organizational capability - not just solving the problem, but creating knowledge for future projects.

## Why It Matters
Smart organizations learn from projects. S23 is about thinking beyond the current project to how your methods improve the organization's future capability.

## What Examiners Want to See
- How your BA approach extends organizational knowledge
- Lessons learned documented for team
- Implications for future projects identified
- Organizational capability improvement through method application

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** B+
**Feedback:** "Strong BA principles applied to complex problem."
**To Improve:** How do methods extend organizational knowledge? Lessons learned for team? Implications for future projects?
