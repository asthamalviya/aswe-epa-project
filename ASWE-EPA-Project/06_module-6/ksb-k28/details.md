# K28: Data Security and Compliance

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding data protection regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2), implementing security controls, and ensuring compliance. Includes encryption, access controls, audit logging, and incident response.

## Why It Matters
Data breaches cost organizations millions. K28 is about building security into systems from the start, not bolting it on after problems occur. Regulators care about intent and effort, not just incident-free records.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Knowledge of relevant regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2 depending on industry)
- Encryption strategy (at-rest and in-transit)
- Access control implementation (RBAC, least privilege)
- Audit logging and monitoring
- Evidence from actual security assessments or compliance audits
