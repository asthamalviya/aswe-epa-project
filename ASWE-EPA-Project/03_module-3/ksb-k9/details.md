# K9: Leadership Principles in Change Management

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding and applying leadership principles to manage organizational change, especially when introducing new data/technology initiatives.

## Why It Matters
Technical solutions fail without good change management. K9 is about recognizing resistance, communicating benefits, and bringing teams along.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Apply specific leadership principles (servant, transformational, situational)
- Address real stakeholder resistance with concrete strategies
- Evidence of actual engagement (not just description)
- Demonstrate how leadership improved adoption/outcomes

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
