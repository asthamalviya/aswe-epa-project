# K9 New Evidence - Apply Leadership Principles to Real Resistance

## The Gap: Name & Apply Specific Leadership Principle to Resistance

Examiners want: "Team resisted X. I used Principle Y specifically. Result: Z."

---

## Leadership Principle #1: Servant Leadership

### Real Resistance Scenario
*"Finance team worried: 'New dashboards mean management sees every error immediately. My job is at risk.'"*

**Servant Leadership Response:**
1. **Listen** - Ask: "What concerns you most?"
2. **Understand** - Hear their fear (not dismiss it)
3. **Act** - Address root concern (create psychological safety)
4. **Support** - Provide training, mentor, safe space

**What You Actually Do:**
- Run listening sessions (Week 1): Hear fears directly from 20 staff
- Create safe feedback channel (Week 2): Anonymous way to voice concerns
- Provide support (Week 3-4): Training, buddy system, error amnesty period
- Build trust: "Errors are learning opportunities, not punishment"

**Result:** 75% adoption vs 45% industry average (servant leadership worked)

---

## Leadership Principle #2: Transformational Leadership

### Real Resistance Scenario
*"Why should I learn new tools? The old way works fine."*

**Transformational Leadership Response:**
1. **Paint Vision** - Show compelling future vs dull present
2. **Make it Appealing** - Connect to what they care about (job security, interesting work)
3. **Involve Early** - Let them shape solution, don't impose it
4. **Celebrate** - Make success visible and rewarded

**What You Actually Do:**
- Vision Workshop (Week 1): "Old way = 4 days/decision. New way = same day. Fast = competitive. Jobs depend on speed."
- Involve Power Users (Week 2): 8 people from different teams design dashboards
- Quick Win (Week 3): Marketing team uses tool, catches trend 3 days early, saves £100k
- Celebrate Publicly (Week 4): Share story in all-hands meeting - suddenly everyone wants dashboard

**Result:** Narrative shifted from "burden" to "competitive advantage"

## Leadership Principle #3: Situational Leadership

### Different People, Different Needs

**Tech-Savvy Staff:**
- Resistance: "This is boring, threatens my job"
- Situational Response: Give them advanced role (architect, trainer)
- Result: Challenged + valued, becomes champion

**Non-Tech Staff:**
- Resistance: "I'll look dumb if I don't understand"
- Situational Response: Beginner training + buddy + pre-built templates
- Result: Confidence builds, adoption increases

**Managers:**
- Resistance: "Training costs too much time"
- Situational Response: Show ROI (5 hours training = 50 hours/month saved)
- Result: Permission granted, team learns
