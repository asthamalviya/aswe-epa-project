# K7 New Evidence - Compare Governance Models

## The Gap: Show You Compared Options

### Model Comparison Table

| Model | How It Works | Pros | Cons | Cost | Best For |
|-------|-------------|------|------|------|----------|
| **Centralized** | Central team makes all decisions | Consistent, clear accountability, easy audit | Slow, bottleneck, inflexible | £180k/year | Regulated industries |
| **Distributed** | Each department decides | Fast, autonomous, flexible | Inconsistent, risky, duplicate effort | £150k/year | Startups, innovation |
| **Hybrid** | Center sets rules, depts implement | Balance consistency + speed | Complex to manage | £160k/year | Growing companies |

### Why You Chose Yours

*"We chose centralized governance because our organization operates in regulated financial services. Data errors trigger compliance violations costing £200k+. The centralized model's consistency (preventing errors) justifies the £180k cost. Speed trade-off is acceptable - strategic stability beats operational speed in our context."*

### Trade-offs You Considered

- **Control vs Speed:** Prioritize control (compliance > agility)
- **Cost vs Consistency:** £180k investment prevents one incident (£200k+ impact)
- **Centralization vs Autonomy:** One standard across org prevents costly rework
