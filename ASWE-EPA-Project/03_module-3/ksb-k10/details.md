# K10: Business Value Creation Through Data Strategy

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how data initiatives create measurable business value - not just implementing tech, but proving it delivers real £ benefits.

## Why It Matters
Many organizations spend on data projects that don't deliver. K10 is about quantifying value, proving ROI, and thinking like a business leader.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Quantified business benefits (costs, revenue, risk reduction)
- ROI calculations and payback periods
- Benefits broken down by type
- Strategic business case, not just cost savings
