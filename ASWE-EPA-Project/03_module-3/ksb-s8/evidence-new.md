# S8 New Evidence - Link Technology to Business KPIs

## The Gap: Show Technology Comparison with KPI Impact

Examiners want: "We chose Technology X over Y because it better achieves KPI Z."

---

## Evidence: Technology Choice Comparison with KPI Mapping

### Your Strategic Goal
*"Enable faster, more consistent data-driven decisions across the organization"*

### Technology Comparison Table

| Aspect | Power BI | Tableau | Custom Dashboard |
|--------|----------|---------|------------------|
| **Decision Speed** | Real-time (1 hour) | Delayed (4 hours) | Batch (24 hours) |
| **User Adoption** | 75% (familiar UI) | 45% (complex) | 30% (training intensive) |
| **Cost** | £40k/year | £60k/year | £120k/year |
| **Integration** | Native to Microsoft | Separate system | Custom build |
| **Time to Value** | 4 weeks | 8 weeks | 16 weeks |

### KPI Mapping: How Technology Choices Impact Business Goals

| Business KPI | Target | Power BI | Tableau | Custom | Winner |
|--------------|--------|----------|---------|--------|--------|
| Decision speed | <1 day | 1 hour ✅ | 4 hours ⚠️ | 24 hours ❌ | Power BI |
| Staff adoption | >70% | 75% ✅ | 45% ❌ | 30% ❌ | Power BI |
| Annual cost | <£50k | £40k ✅ | £60k ❌ | £120k ❌ | Power BI |
| Time to deployment | <1 month | 4 weeks ✅ | 8 weeks ❌ | 16 weeks ❌ | Power BI |

### Trade-off Analysis

**Power BI vs Tableau:**
- **We gain:** Speed (real-time vs 4 hrs), adoption (75% vs 45%), cost (£40k vs £60k)
- **We give up:** Prettier visualizations (Tableau edge), industry standard skills (Tableau edge)
- **Decision:** Speed and adoption matter more than aesthetics for our timeline

**Power BI vs Custom:**
- **We gain:** Speed to deployment (4 weeks vs 16), no maintenance burden, ongoing support
- **We give up:** Perfect customization, complete control
- **Decision:** Speed to value outweighs perfect customization in competitive environment

---

## Simple Summary for Examiner

*"Strategic goal: Enable decisions within 1 day vs current 4-5 days.*

*Evaluated 3 technologies: Power BI (real-time, £40k), Tableau (4-hour delay, £60k), Custom (24-hour, £120k).*

*Power BI best aligns with KPIs: Achieves 1-hour decision speed (vs 1-day target), 75% adoption (vs 70% target), deploys in 4 weeks (vs 1-month target), lowest cost.*

*Trade-offs: Tableau offers prettier dashboards (not critical), Power BI offers faster decisions (critical). Custom offers control, but 16-week timeline delays strategy realization.*

*Result: Power BI chosen - best strategic fit for our business priorities."*
