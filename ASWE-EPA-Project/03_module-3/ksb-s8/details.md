# S8: Strategic Thinking - Data & Technology Alignment

## What This KSB Is About
Ability to think strategically about technology choices and ensure they support business goals, with clear ROI and measurable outcomes.

## Why It Matters
Good technology choices fail without strategic alignment. S8 is about connecting tech decisions to business KPIs, not just picking cool tools.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Link technology choices to measurable business outcomes (KPIs)
- Compare alternative technologies with trade-off analysis
- Show ROI or business value of each choice
- Strategic reasoning, not just technical merits
