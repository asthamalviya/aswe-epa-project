# B8: Inclusive Practice Behaviours

## What This KSB Is About
Demonstrating inclusive behaviours in practice - actively engaging diverse stakeholders, considering different perspectives, ensuring voices are heard.

## Why It Matters
Inclusion isn't a checkbox - it's how you actually work. B8 is about evidence of inclusive practice, not just intentions.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Active engagement with diverse stakeholder groups
- Evidence of considering different perspectives
- How diverse input shaped your solution
- Inclusive communication and collaboration

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** B+
**Feedback:** "Comprehensive solutions with specific examples demonstrating work."
**To Improve:** Evidence of actual inclusive practice, not just described. Show how different voices influenced decisions.
