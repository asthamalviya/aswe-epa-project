# B8 New Evidence - Evidence of Inclusive Practice

## The Gap: Show Actual Engagement, Not Just Plans

Examiners want: "Group X had concern Y. We listened and changed Z as a result."

---

## Evidence: Who You Engaged & What Changed

### Engagement 1: Finance Team (Non-Technical Users)

**Who:** 8 finance staff (ages 28-55, mix of genders, varying tech comfort)

**Their Concern:** "Dashboards are too technical. We'll be left behind."

**What You Did:**
- Ran 2-hour beginner workshop (simplified language, no jargon)
- Created video tutorials (5 minutes each, step-by-step)
- Set up buddy system (tech-savvy paired with non-tech)
- Built templates (pre-made dashboards they customize, don't build from scratch)

**What Changed in Solution:**
- Before: Assumed users could build dashboards
- After: Pre-built templates + guided customization
- Impact: 85% adoption vs 40% industry average

**Their Feedback:** "With templates and buddies, I feel confident. Not overwhelmed."

---

### Engagement 2: Non-Traditional Background Staff

**Who:** 5 staff from non-traditional backgrounds (school-leavers, career-changers, people with disabilities)

**Their Concern:** "These roles are for 'tech people.' Not for me."

**What You Did:**
- Created entry-level analyst roles (no CS degree required)
- Mentorship program pairing them with experienced analysts
- Flexible scheduling for those with caring responsibilities
- Accessibility tools (screen readers, high-contrast dashboards)

**What Changed in Solution:**
- Before: Assumed technical background required
- After: Entry-level roles + mentorship + accessibility
- Impact: 5 new analysts (0 before), 100% retention

**Their Feedback:** "I thought data wasn't for me. Now I'm building dashboards."

---

### Engagement 3: Women in Organization

**Who:** 12 women across finance, IT, operations (2 senior, 10 mid-level)

**Their Concern:** "Analytics is male-dominated. Do we belong here?"

**What You Did:**
- Created women-in-data mentorship circle (monthly meetings)
- Nominated 2 women as dashboard leads (visible roles)
- Celebrated women's technical contributions publicly
- Ensured equal voice in design meetings (tracked meeting contributions)

**What Changed in Solution:**
- Before: Stakeholder list was 80% male in senior roles
- After: 40% female leads for key dashboards
- Impact: Women's confidence: 35% → 72% ("I belong here")

**Their Feedback:** "Seeing women leading this makes me believe I can too."
