# S23: Business Analysis & Project Management

## What This KSB Is About
Skill in applying BA and PM methodologies to solve real organizational problems - not just picking frameworks, but using them effectively.

## Why It Matters
Many organizations have BA/PM frameworks but don't apply them well. S23 is about using tools strategically to solve complex problems.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Multiple BA/PM methodologies evaluated and compared
- Clear justification for choices made
- Critical analysis of frameworks (strengths AND limitations)
- Methodologies applied effectively to actual problem

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** B+
**Feedback:** "Strong understanding of BA and PM principles applied well to complex organizational problem."
**To Improve:** More critical analysis of framework limitations. Discuss potential weaknesses. Explore trade-offs between approaches.
