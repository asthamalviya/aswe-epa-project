# B7: Critical Analysis Behaviours

## What This KSB Is About
Demonstrating critical thinking - evaluating your own solutions honestly, identifying weaknesses, and proposing improvements.

## Why It Matters
Anyone can describe their solution. Critical thinking shows maturity - recognizing what could be better.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Honest evaluation of your approach
- Discussion of limitations and trade-offs
- Alternative approaches considered and rejected
- Realistic assessment of what worked and what didn't
