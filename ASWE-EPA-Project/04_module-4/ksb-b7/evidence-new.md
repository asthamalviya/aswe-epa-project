# B7 New Evidence - Critical Evaluation of Your Solution

## The Gap: Be Honest About Weaknesses

Examiners want: "This approach works BUT has risks/limitations that are..."

## Honest Critical Analysis

### What You'd Acknowledge

**Risk 1: Agile Works Well for Tech Teams, Less for Non-Tech**
- Your team has tech background
- Finance team less familiar with iterative delivery
- Risk: Misalignment on progress definition
- Mitigation: Weekly demos in business language, not sprint jargon

**Risk 2: Agile Needs Strong Product Owner**
- You have a good one currently
- If they leave = project could derail
- Risk: Knowledge concentration
- Mitigation: Document all product decisions, train backup

**Risk 3: Scope Creep in Agile**
- Flexible requirements can expand infinitely
- Risk: Budget/timeline overrun
- Mitigation: Fixed budget, added features only if lower priority removed

---

### What Didn't Go Perfectly

**What Worked:**
✓ Sprint rhythm (2 weeks = good pace)
✓ Stakeholder engagement (weekly demos)
✓ Team velocity stable by Week 4

**What Struggled:**
✗ First 3 sprints had estimation errors (fixed in Sprint 4)
✗ Retrospectives initially surface-level (improved with coaching)
✗ Documentation initially sacrificed (added to Definition of Done)

**What You'd Do Differently:**
- Start with framework agreement (Definition of Done) before Sprint 1
- Have retrospective coach (not just self-facilitated)
- Pair estimation with historical data, not just planning poker

---

## Trade-Offs You Made (and Why)

### Trade-off 1: Speed vs Documentation
- **Speed:** Agile sprints, fast delivery (favors speed)
- **Documentation:** Would slow down (favors thoroughness)
- **Your choice:** Speed initially, then retrospectively added documentation
- **Would you choose same way again?** "Yes, but start documentation earlier"

### Trade-off 2: Flexibility vs Predictability
- **Flexibility:** Agile allows changes mid-project
- **Predictability:** Waterfall promises fixed scope/timeline
- **Your choice:** Flexibility (matched organization's reality)
- **Honest assessment:** "This cost us 2 weeks timeline drift, worth it for 3 mid-course corrections that added value"

### Trade-off 3: Team Autonomy vs Governance
- **Autonomy:** Agile teams make daily decisions
- **Governance:** Compliance needs central oversight
- **Your choice:** Autonomy with monthly governance review
- **Risk acknowledged:** "If compliance didn't flag issues monthly, we could drift non-compliant"
