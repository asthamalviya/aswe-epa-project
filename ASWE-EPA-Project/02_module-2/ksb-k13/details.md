# K13: Implement Data Analytics & Engineering Solutions

## What This KSB Is About
Ability to design and build data analytics or machine learning solutions that are relevant to business needs, comply with standards, and can be tested for performance.

## Why It Matters
Having data is useless without tools to extract insights. K13 is about practical implementation - turning business problems into working solutions.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Working prototype that addresses real business need
- Compliance with internal and external standards (ethical, legal, social)
- Testing evidence showing model/solution performance
- Evidence of debugging and data cleaning
- Connection to business requirements
