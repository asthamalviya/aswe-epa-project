# S10: Design, Implement & Debug Data Product

## What This KSB Is About
Practical skill in building complete data products - from design through implementation to testing and fixing problems.

## Why It Matters
Anyone can design something on paper. S10 is about real implementation - handling edge cases, debugging issues, and delivering working products.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Sophisticated ML models with multiple techniques
- Strategic alignment with business needs
- Detailed analysis of model performance (scores, metrics)
- Evaluation of whether performance is good enough to deploy
- Mapping model scores to business KPIs
- Evidence of debugging and refinement
