# S11: Realize Value from Data Solutions

## What This KSB Is About
Practical skill in implementing data solutions and demonstrating they deliver measurable value - moving from theory to actual business outcomes.

## Why It Matters
The difference between a good analyst and great analyst is ability to follow through - ensuring solutions actually work in practice and deliver promised benefits.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Evidence of implementing solution (not just designing it)
- Measurement of outcomes against goals
- Stakeholder feedback showing value realized
- Continuous improvement based on feedback

## Grade Feedback from Examiner
**Your Grade:** B+
**Feedback:** "Good work converting prototype to measurable value. Working prototype relevant to business needs."
**To Improve:** Show actual implementation metrics and stakeholder feedback. Evidence of value realized, not just promised.
