# K14: Demonstrate Value Realization from Data Solutions

## What This KSB Is About
Understanding how to measure and demonstrate the actual business value created by data initiatives - not just implementing technology, but proving it delivers real benefits.

## Why It Matters
Many organizations spend money on data projects that don't deliver value. K14 is about articulating, measuring, and proving benefits to stakeholders.

## What Examiners Want to See
- Comprehensive value analysis of your solution
- Quantified impact (£ savings, revenue, risk reduction)
- Specific examples of how solution impacts teams (finance, operations, etc.)
- Communication plan showing how you'd persuade stakeholders
