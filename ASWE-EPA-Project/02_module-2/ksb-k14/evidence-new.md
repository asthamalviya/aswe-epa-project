## The Gap: Turn "Benefits" into Numbers

Examiners want to see: "Before: £X cost/risk. After: £Y. Benefit: £Z."

---

## Quantified Value Analysis Template

### Value Type 1: Cost Savings (Time/Effort Reduction)

**Current State (Manual Process):**
- Credit analyst reviews 20 companies/day
- Each review takes 4 hours (financial analysis, risk assessment)
- Team of 5 analysts
- Annual cost: 5 × 20 × 4 × 250 days × £45/hour = £450k/year in labor

**Future State (With ML Model):**
- Analyst reviews 30 companies/day (model pre-screens)
- Each review takes 1.5 hours (model identified key risks)
- Same team of 5 analysts
- Annual cost: 5 × 30 × 1.5 × 250 days × £45/hour = £253k/year

**Benefit:** £450k - £253k = **£197k annual labor savings**

---

### Value Type 2: Risk Reduction (Prevented Losses)

**Current State (Missed Insolvencies):**
- Manual review catches 60% of insolvencies
- 40% slip through (analyzed after company already failed)
- Average loss per missed insolvency: £100k (uncollected credit)
- Industry avg: 40 insolvencies/year in portfolio
- Annual risk: 40 × 0.4 × £100k = **£1.6M potential loss**

**Future State (With ML Model):**
- ML catches 87% of insolvencies early
- Combined with expert review: 95% catch rate
- Only 2 per year slip through
- Annual risk: 40 × 0.05 × £100k = **£200k potential loss**

**Benefit:** £1.6M - £200k = **£1.4M risk reduction**

---

### Value Type 3: Revenue Growth (Opportunity Capture)

**Current State (Reactive):**
- Slow decisions mean lost first-mover advantage
- Marketing identifies trend on Day 10
- By time credit approves, competitors already moving
- Estimated lost revenue/opportunities: £250k/year

**Future State (Proactive with Real-Time Model):**
- Model flags trends in real-time (Day 2)
- Credit can decide within 24 hours
- Company is first to market
- Estimated captured revenue: £180k/year

**Benefit:** **£180k additional annual revenue**
