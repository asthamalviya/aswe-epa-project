## How grading works

- Each assessment method (AM) is graded Fail, Pass or Distinction. There is no Merit at AM level.
- A Pass requires every Pass criterion for that AM. A Distinction requires every Pass and every Distinction criterion.
- Several KSBs share one criterion (shown in brackets after the criterion). Evidence for a shared criterion must cover every KSB listed.
- Only 4 AM2 KSBs have Distinction criteria (K19, K20, S15, S20). AM1 has 9 (S1, K5, S5, K18, S13, K25, S17, S18, S22).

| AM1: Report and presentation | AM2: Portfolio discussion | Overall EPA grade |
|---|---|---|
| Fail | Any | Fail |
| Any | Fail | Fail |
| Pass | Pass | Pass |
| Pass | Distinction | Merit |
| Distinction | Pass | Merit |
| Distinction | Distinction | Distinction |

The degree letter grade depends on AM1 Distinction criteria met, with every AM1 Pass criterion and an AM2 Pass as the baseline: D (none), C (3), B (5), A (6). The brief also lists A for all 9. U means a failed KSB or failed AM2; F means the capstone was not submitted on time or the assessment did not take place.
