## Evidence Quality Levels

| Level | Characteristics |
|-------|----------------|
| **Distinction** | Critical evaluation of options, clear impact metrics, reflective learning, sophisticated technical choices |
| **Merit** | Justification of decisions, analysis of alternatives, clear outcomes, good structure |
| **Pass** | Competency described, work explained, KSB link made, meets requirements |
