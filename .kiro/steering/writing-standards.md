# Writing Standards for EPA Submissions

Apply these standards whenever helping write or improve content for the Capstone report, portfolio evidence, or presentation.

## Voice and Tone

- Write in **first person** ("I designed...", "I chose...", "I implemented...")
- Be **specific** — avoid vague generalisations
- Sound like a **practising software engineer**, not a student essay
- Professional but not overly formal

## The Distinction Difference

The gap between Pass and Distinction is almost always **evaluation vs description**.

| Pass level | Distinction level |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "I used React for the frontend" | "I chose React over Vue.js because the team had prior expertise and React's virtual DOM suited our frequent state updates" |
| "The project was completed on time" | "By implementing daily standups and a Kanban board to surface blockers early, I kept the project on schedule despite a mid-sprint scope change" |
| "I wrote unit tests" | "I achieved 87% test coverage using Jest, focusing on edge cases in the business logic layer. I discovered a critical off-by-one error that would have caused incorrect billing calculations" |
| "I learned a lot" | "This project challenged my assumptions about microservices — the operational overhead was higher than anticipated, which would inform my architecture decisions in future projects" |

## Sentence Patterns to Use

**Decision-making:**
- "I chose X over Y because..."
- "After evaluating X, Y, and Z against the criteria of [performance/cost/maintainability], I selected..."
- "This decision was informed by [requirement/constraint/research]..."

**Technical depth:**
- "The trade-off here was between..."
- "This approach has the limitation of... which I mitigated by..."
- "In hindsight, I would have considered... because..."

**Impact:**
- "This resulted in [metric]..."
- "The business impact was..."
- "Stakeholders reported that..."

**KSB linking:**
- "This demonstrates K19 because I applied the Data Protection Act and accessibility standards to..."
- "This evidences S4 because I initiated, designed, coded, tested and debugged the component..."

## What to Avoid

- "We did..." (always "I did..." — your contribution must be visible)
- "It was decided that..." (passive voice hides your agency)
- "I think/feel/believe..." (replace with "I evaluated/assessed/determined...")
- Jargon without explanation (explain technical terms briefly)
- Lists without explanation (every bullet should have a "because" or "which meant that")
